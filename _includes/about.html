关于About

我是张一鸣，南洋理工大学（NTU）电子电气工程学院工学硕士在读。我的研究兴趣是 MLLM 与 Agent，关注多模态大语言模型的理解与推理， 以及智能体的规划与工具使用。

I am Yiming Zhang, an MEng student in the School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). My research interests lie in MLLM and Agent, focusing on the understanding and reasoning of multimodal large language models, as well as the planning and tool use of agents.

我本科就读于北京邮电大学人工智能学院。在新加坡期间，我先后与 Zhongzheng Qiao 在时间序列方向、与 Bo Zhao 在 MLLM 的时空理解方向开展合作。 2025 年 6 月起，我在华为新加坡诺亚方舟实验室实习，导师为 Kai Han，我们主要研究多模态隐空间推理；同期也与华为港研所合作，关注原生 MLLMs。 2026 年 6 月起，我在蚂蚁集团（杭州）实习至今。

I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). During my time in Singapore, I collaborated with Zhongzheng Qiao on time series and with Bo Zhao on the spatial-temporal understanding of MLLMs. From June 2025, I interned at the Huawei Singapore Noah's Ark Lab under the supervision of Kai Han, where we mainly worked on multimodal latent-space reasoning; in parallel, I also collaborated with the Huawei Hong Kong Research Center on native MLLMs. Since June 2026, I have been an intern at Ant Group (Hangzhou).
